Loops

The thoughts that repeat. Uncertainty. Chaos. Fear. Always, what if?

"What if I'm wrong about everything I think I know about myself?"

This one comes back every few months. Usually after a conversation that goes deeper than expected, or after watching someone else seem so sure of who they are.

"What if I waited too long, and now it's too late?"

For the decision. The message. The version of my life I kept deferring. I don't always know what the thing is, just the feeling that a window is closing.

"What if they remember me differently than I remember myself?"

The gap between who we are and who we seem is unsettling. I go back over things I said and try to see them from the outside.

"What if nothing I'm afraid of actually matters?"

This one is less anxiety and more exhaustion. What if all this weight I've been carrying is just optional?
